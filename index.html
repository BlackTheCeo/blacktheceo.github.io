Welcome to Your Neighborhood Bakery

At North Star Bakery, we bring the community together through the timeless art of traditional baking. Every morning before dawn, our bakers mix organic, locally sourced grains, natural sourdough cultures, and simple ingredients to bake fresh breads and pastries for our neighbors, commuters, and families.

Daily Specialties

  • 36-Hour Sourdough Loaf: Naturally leavened, crisp crust, and soft interior.
  • French Butter Croissants: Flaky, golden-brown layers baked fresh every morning.
  • Custom Cakes & Seasonal Pastries: Made to order for special events and holidays.
